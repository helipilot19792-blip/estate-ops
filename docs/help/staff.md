# Cleaner And Grounds Portal Help

Cleaner and grounds portals are focused on daily work, job response, property details, reporting issues, chat, and the Bulletin Board.

## Cleaner Portal

- Active Jobs shows cleaning work waiting for response, offered with a deadline, accepted, or in progress.
- History shows previous cleaning jobs.
- Open a job card to see timing, property, notes, access details, SOPs, photos, teammates when enabled, and the turnover checklist.
- Accept or decline work so management knows availability.
- An offer countdown shows when a response deadline applies. An unanswered offer can expire and move to another eligible cleaner.
- Accepted work can appear in the cleaner calendar, and supported reminder emails can include a calendar link.
- Start and complete the checklist so admins can see job progress. Checklist completion does not replace reporting damage or maintenance issues.
- Report Issue is used for damage, missing supplies, access trouble, safety concerns, and other property problems.
- Report Issue can include photos from the device camera or the camera roll/file picker when supported by the device and browser.
- Chat is for quick questions with management.
- Bulletin Board is for team-wide updates shared with admins, cleaners, and grounds.

## Grounds Portal

- Active Jobs shows exterior or grounds work waiting for response or already accepted.
- History shows previous grounds jobs.
- Job details may include service windows, notes, property details, access details, SOPs, and photos.
- Accept or decline work so management can route the work correctly.
- Report Issue is used for exterior concerns, hazards, access trouble, damage, or other property problems.
- Report Issue can include photos from the device camera or the camera roll/file picker when supported by the device and browser.
- Chat is for quick questions with management.
- Bulletin Board is for team-wide updates shared with admins, cleaners, and grounds.

## Assignments And Job Offers

Staff only see work available to the cleaner or grounds account connected to their signed-in profile and organization.

- Property assignments determine which staff accounts are eligible for work.
- Cleaning offers can follow priority, training rotation, or manual assignment rules chosen by the admin.
- Declining an offer records the response and can allow the job to advance to another eligible person.
- The app can prevent unsafe same-day cleaning conflicts when the cleaner already has accepted work.
- An admin can accept or decline an eligible cleaning offer on a cleaner's behalf when operationally necessary. The cleaner should confirm unexpected changes with management.
- A cleaner can request release from future accepted work. The admin reviews the request before affected jobs are released or reassigned.

## Cleaner Payments

Admins can record cleaner payout amounts and payment status in an internal ledger. The cleaner portal does not expose property payout rates or the admin's payment ledger. Ask management directly about payment questions.

## Alerts

Each staff member must enable alerts on the device and browser they use. If alerts were enabled before a VAPID key rotation, the user may need to turn alerts off and back on.

## My Account

Cleaners and grounds users can open My Account from the top portal bar. They can review their signed-in email, role, and account ID, then update their full name, phone number, password, or preferred language.

My Account also lets staff request deletion of their account and personal information. This creates a request for review rather than instantly deleting operational records. Some job, safety, billing, or business records may need to be retained.

## Common Staff Questions

### I do not see a job I expected.

Check that you are signed into the correct portal, organization, and account. Admins should confirm the property assignment, account membership, job staffing state, response deadline, prior decline, and any accepted same-day conflict.

### Why did an offered job disappear?

The offer may have expired, been declined, been accepted by another eligible staff member, or been reassigned by an admin. Ask management to check the job's staffing and notification history.

### How do I show that cleaning is underway?

Open the accepted job, start the work when that control is available, and complete the turnover checklist items as they are finished. Report damage, missing supplies, or safety issues separately with Report Issue.

### Can I see my payout rate?

No. Cleaner payout rates and the admin payment ledger are not shown in the cleaner portal. Contact management for payment details.

### Should I use chat or report issue?

Use Report Issue when something needs to be tracked as a property problem. Use Chat for a quick question, clarification, or conversation.

### What is Bulletin Board for?

Use Bulletin Board when you need to read updates shared with the full field team. It is not the same as Chat and should not create a normal chat conversation.

### I reported an issue and it kept saying Reporting.

If the issue does not finish sending, check that the staff account is linked to the correct cleaner or grounds account for that organization and property. If the report eventually fails, ask an admin to confirm that the property assignment and staff account membership are set up correctly.

### Can I attach a photo from my phone?

Yes. The issue flow supports adding photos. Depending on the device and browser, this may appear as a camera option, a photo library option, or a file picker.
