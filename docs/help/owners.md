# Owner Portal Help

The owner portal gives owners a focused view of their property activity, invoices, booking insights, maintenance visibility, and chat with management.

## Main Areas

- Overview: Current owner-facing property activity, upcoming work, and timeline.
- Insights: Booking activity and occupancy-style information from synced calendars.
- Invoices: Sent and paid invoices, quotes or statements made available to the owner, downloadable documents, totals, tax lines, and payment instructions.
- Chat: Conversation with property management.

## Invoices

Owners can view financial documents that have been sent to them. Draft or running invoices are not owner-facing until the admin sends the finished document. Documents may be generated by the system or uploaded by an admin. Invoice line items can include turnover work, grounds work, expenses, tax lines, notes, and payment instructions.

- Use the available download control to save the invoice or statement PDF.
- A statement summarizes saved invoice history for a selected property and date range; it is not a new charge by itself.
- A quote is a proposal and does not mean the work or payment has been approved unless management confirms it.
- Payment state and reminder history are controlled by management. Contact the manager if a payment status looks incorrect.

## Maintenance Visibility

Admins control what maintenance flags are visible to owners. Owners may see issue history when it has been shared with them.

## My Account

Owners can open My Account from the top portal bar. My Account shows the signed-in email, role, and account ID. Owners can update their full name, phone number, password, or preferred language from there.

My Account also lets owners request deletion of their account and personal information. This creates a request for review rather than instantly deleting operational records. Some invoice, property, legal, security, or business records may need to be retained.

## Common Owner Questions

### Where do I ask about an invoice?

Use Owner Chat and mention the invoice number or property name.

### Why do I not see a draft invoice or quote?

Owners only see documents the admin has made available or sent. Ask management whether the document is still a draft.

### Can I download an invoice or statement?

Yes, when a downloadable document is available, open it in Invoices and use the download control.

### Why do I not see a property?

An admin must link the owner account to the property. Admins can manage this from the owner/property access setup.
